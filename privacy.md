# Iris42 Shift Bid — SMS Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 3, 2026_

Iris42 Shift Bid is a personal shift-scheduling tool operated by an individual (contact: iris42hb@gmail.com). It sends SMS text messages only to colleagues who have voluntarily opted in, for the sole purpose of coordinating work shift-bid selections. It is a personal tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated on behalf of any employer, agency, or organization. These messages contain no marketing or promotional content.

## No sharing of mobile information

No mobile information — including mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, and messaging consent — is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with, sold to, rented to, or transferred to any third parties or affiliates for any purpose. Mobile numbers are used only to deliver shift-scheduling notifications to people who have opted in.

## Information we collect and how it is used

We collect only a name and a mobile phone number, solely to send shift-bid scheduling text notifications. This information is not used for any other purpose.

## Message frequency

Message frequency is low — typically about one message per participant per bid cycle, with only a few bid cycles per year.

## Rates

Message and data rates may apply.

## Opting out and help

Reply **STOP** to any message to opt out at any time. Reply **HELP** for help.

## Contact

Questions about these messages or this policy can be directed to iris42hb@gmail.com.

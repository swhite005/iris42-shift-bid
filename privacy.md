# HBPD Shift Bid — SMS Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 3, 2026_

The Huntington Beach Police Department (HBPD) sends SMS text messages to department sergeants for the sole purpose of internal shift-bid scheduling. These messages contain no marketing or promotional content.

## No sharing of mobile information

No mobile information — including mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, and messaging consent — is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with, sold to, rented to, or transferred to any third parties or affiliates for any purpose. Mobile numbers are collected and maintained internally and are used only to deliver shift-scheduling notifications to department employees.

## Information we collect and how it is used

We collect only the employee's name and mobile phone number, solely to send shift-bid scheduling text notifications. We do not use this information for any other purpose.

## Message frequency

Message frequency is low — typically about one message per sergeant per bid cycle, with only a few bid cycles per year.

## Rates

Message and data rates may apply.

## Opting out and help

Reply **STOP** to any message to opt out at any time. Reply **HELP** for help.

## Contact

Questions about these messages or this policy can be directed to SWhite@hbpd.org.
